What changed in v2
  • Dashed outline on the right marks exactly what the embed draws: from the first day heading down to “Schedule by fair.yoga”. Everything above it (“Classes with Anna”) is the teacher's own page.
  • Two cards instead of one: “Your schedule” (weeks ahead, studio classes) is saved on the server and applies to every embed and the feed; “Look” only changes the copied code.
  • Class type and location filters are gone (not in v1).
  • “Your site's” style hides the Colours row, drops color-scheme from the code, and the preview shows a bare list with a note.
